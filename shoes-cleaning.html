CARE CAPABILITIES

How We Care For Your Shoes

A strict, standardized process executed manually by trained shoe technicians.

01. Individual Hand Wash

We do not use automatic washing machines. Every shoe is hand-brushed inside out using specialized soft boar/horsehair brushes to protect delicate fabrics.

02. Organic Solvents

We clean with top-tier leather care chemicals (Saphir and Tarrago) which clean deep stains without stripping the leather's natural color or oils.

03. Controlled Temperature Drying

Shoes are placed in dark dehumidifying rooms at standard temperatures. No high-heat is used, preventing fabric contraction and sole glue cracking.

04. UVC Ray Disinfection

Finally, we sanitize all shoes using UVC ultraviolet ray cabins to eliminate 99.9% of internal bacteria, fungal spores, and foot odors.
