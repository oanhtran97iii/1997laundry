Dry Clean Only Warning

Our standard weight-based laundry (per kg) utilizes cold-water wash and 60°C tumble drying. This is NOT suitable for delicate items. Suits, wool, cashmere, silk, down jackets, and leather garments MUST be dry cleaned to prevent permanent shrinkage, warping, or loss of natural sheen. Express turnaround is not available for dry cleaning due to the multi-stage solvent extraction process required (Standard 3-day turnaround).
