Why Our Pricing is Different

Unlike others, one flat price at 1997 covers our entire premium dry cleaning process—with absolutely no hidden fees.

01. Stain Pre-Treatment

Collar grime, underarm sweat, sleeve cuffs, or minor food and wine stains are meticulously spot-treated by hand before washing—at no extra charge.

02. Hydrocarbon Solvents

We dry clean in individual loads using Hydrocarbon solvents that are odorless, skin-safe, biodegradable, and protect garments from shrinking.

03. Sculptural Steam Press

Professional hand-pressing using high-pressure vertical steam tables restores original shape, sharp lapels, crisp trouser creases, and custom silhouette flow.

04. Neat Hanger Wrap

Garments are delivered hanging upright on high-quality hangers, sealed in premium dust bags to completely prevent transit wrinkles—ready to wear.
