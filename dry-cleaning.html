Dry Clean Only Warning

Our standard weight-based laundry (giặt kg) utilizes cold-water wash and 60°C tumble drying. This is NOT suitable for delicate items. Suits, wool, cashmere, silk, down jackets, and leather garments MUST be dry cleaned to prevent permanent shrinkage, warping, or loss of natural sheen. Express turnaround is not available for dry cleaning due to the multi-stage solvent extraction process required (Standard 3-day turnaround).

SERVICE FLOW

The 3-Day Organic Dry Cleaning Process

A slow, highly controlled process to guarantee maximum safety and restoration for premium garments.

01. Detailed Inspection

Technicians inspect every garment, cover delicate buttons with protective foil, and pre-treat individual grease/ink stains by hand.

02. Organic Solvents

Garments are washed in closed-loop dry cleaning machines using premium eco-solvents that dissolve oils without affecting natural wool, silk, or feathers.

03. Controlled De-solvent

Drying is performed inside the chamber under slow temperature changes. No hot tumble drying is used, protecting shape lining structures from warping.

04. Meticulous Hand Press

Our experts use special form-finishers and vertical steam tables to restore fabric loft, iron collars/lapels sharp, and wrap garments in dust bags.
