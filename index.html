COMMON Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries about our laundry services, rates, and shipping policies.

Standard Wash & Fold starts from 105,000 VND for up to 3kg (24h turnaround). Our Same-Day Express combo is 180,000 VND for up to 5kg, and the 4-Hour Super Express combo is 220,000 VND for up to 5kg. Other specialty services like dry cleaning, shoe cleaning, and steam press are priced per item.

Standard weight-based laundry turnaround is 24 hours. Express laundry combos can be delivered back in 4 or 6 hours. Specialty services like dry cleaning, shoe care, or curtains require 1 to 3 days depending on fabric delicate care.

Our weight-based Wash & Fold service (by kg) is tumble dried and folded; it does not include ironing. If you need ironing, please check our Steam Press service. Also, please note that we dry clothes at 60°C; natural cotton, linen, or wool may shrink, so we highly recommend choosing Dry Cleaning or Steam Press per piece for these fabrics to avoid any heat damage.

We offer pickup & delivery across inner HCMC districts. The standard delivery fee is 20,000 VND/way for distances under 3km, and +8,000 VND/km for distances exceeding 3km. We offer free pickup & delivery for orders over 1,000,000 VND.

The easiest and fastest way is to text our friendly customer service team on WhatsApp or Zalo at 0866.137.043. We will confirm your address, service type, and coordinate a pickup time immediately.
