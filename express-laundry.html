MUST-KNOW GUIDELINES

Some Important Information

Please read through our service policies and process guidelines carefully.

Express Service Availability

We offer premium express service. If you require immediate or same-day turnaround, please contact us directly via WhatsApp or Zalo for instant routing.

Washing Temperature & Natural Fabrics

Our standard cleaning process includes a cold-water wash and tumble drying at 60°C. Please be advised that certain natural materials (such as wool, linen, or high-percentage cotton) may shrink under standard tumble drying. As space is limited, standard air-drying is not available in our shop. However, we can accommodate hang-drying for fewer than 5 items upon request.

Stubborn Stains & Treatment

Tough stains such as old marks, mold, color bleeding, or deep grease may not be fully removed with standard regular washing. We can apply additional professional stain-releasing treatments to these specific areas upon request; however, complete stain removal results cannot be 100% guaranteed. Please inform our staff in advance if you would like this service.

Hotel Reception Collection & Delivery

Our delivery staff can collect and deliver your laundry bags directly at your hotel's reception desk. If you’d like us to pick up or return your laundry at reception, please provide your room number and full name so the receptionist can verify details before handing over bags.

Multiple Bags & Group Sorting

If you have multiple laundry bags and want each bag washed separately (e.g., for a group, families, or white/color division), please let us know in advance to avoid clothes mixing. Please note that if you request each bag to be washed separately, each bag will be charged independently as a separate order based on the service tier you select.

Standard Delivery Time

If you are not using our super express service, all standard laundry orders will be completed and returned within 24 to 36 hours from the time we receive them.
