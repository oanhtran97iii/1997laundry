MUST-KNOW GUIDELINES

Some Important Information

Please read through our service policies and process guidelines carefully.

Express Service Availability

Need same-day or super express service? Contact us directly via WhatsApp or Zalo for instant pickup and priority processing.

Standard Wash & Dry Policy (Dry Clean Only Warnings)

Our standard laundry uses cold wash & 60°C tumble-dry. This process is not suitable for delicate garments and will cause shrinkage or damage:

Need Dry Cleaning?

Please request our professional, piece-based dry cleaning instead:

  • Standard 3-day turnaround
  • Cleaned & steam pressed
  • Express is not available
DO NOT Wash & Dry:
  • Suits & Coats (warps linings)
  • Wool & Cashmere (severe shrinkage)
  • Silk, Satin & Rayon (loses shape/sheen)
  • Leather & Suede (cracks & peels)
  • Embellished Items (beads & lace tear)
  • Down Jackets (feathers clump)

Stubborn Stains & Treatment

Deep stains (grease, ink, mold, color bleeding) require special care. For complete whitening and stain removal, we charge per item (piece-based) and require 3 days to process. If you do not have enough time, we can perform a quick pre-brushing treatment instead, though please note it may not remove the stains completely.

Hotel Reception Collection & Delivery

We can collect and return your laundry at hotel reception. Please provide your full name and room number in advance to authorize reception handover.

Multiple Bags & Group Sorting

To wash bags separately (for white/color sorting or groups), please notify us in advance. Each separate wash request is billed as an independent order.

Standard Delivery Time

Standard orders (non-express) are processed and returned within 24 to 36 hours from pickup.
