SERVICE FLOW

Standardized Cleaning & Pressing

Every single item is individually cared for by hand to ensure pristine results.

01. Fabric Sorting

Clothes are separated by material and colors. Stains are pre-brushed manually using gentle eco-removers before clean cycle.

02. Piece-Based Wash

We wash items using cold water and specialized fabric conditioners, protecting threads and colors from warp/stretch.

03. Low-Temp Drying

Garments are dried at controlled, low temperatures in dehumidified chambers, preventing shrinkage and color bleeding.

04. Meticulous Steam Press

Using vertical steam irons, our experts iron out creases, preserve shapes, inspect details, and pack items on hangers.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our steam pressing and per-piece laundry? Here are our answers.

Standard weight-based laundry (giặt sấy theo kg) is machine-washed and machine-tumble dried together without ironing. Laundry per piece (giặt tính chiếc) is cared for individually, uses gentle cold-water washing, and includes professional vertical steam press ironing for each item.

Our standard turnaround for piece-based washing and steam pressing is 2 to 3 days. This ensures every garment is meticulously treated, completely air-dried under controlled temperature, and vertically steam ironed perfectly before delivery.
